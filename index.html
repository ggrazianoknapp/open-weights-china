Open-weight models · 2026

The open frontier is Chinese.

The models anyone can download and look inside now mostly come from China. They sit about four months behind the closed frontier, several were trained on its outputs, and the labs that make them share a campus with China's safety researchers.

This page puts numbers on each of those claims.

      Why it matters

      If you want to read a frontier model's internals, you are reading a Chinese checkpoint

      Interpretability needs weights. Probes, SAEs and attribution all run on activations, and closed models do not give you any.

      The open models near the frontier are Chinese, and several were trained on the closed frontier's outputs. So a Chinese open model is the closest thing to a readable copy of a closed US model, and the distillation lineage is what makes the copy faithful.

      That makes measurement of deception, evaluation awareness and inherited traits a question about Chinese weights. The people who built them, and a growing share of the people studying them, are in Beijing and Shanghai.

      The window may be narrowing. Flagships are going API-only. The time to calibrate open readers against closed authors is while frontier-adjacent weights are still downloadable.
