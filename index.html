The open frontier is Chinese

The models anyone can download and look inside now mostly come from China. They sit about four months behind the closed frontier, and several were trained on its outputs.

This page puts a number on each of those claims, with a source for every one.

      Why it matters

      Interpretability needs weights

      1

      Probes run on activations

      SAEs, linear probes and attribution all need the weights. Closed models give you none. The open models near the frontier are Chinese.

      2

      Lineage makes the copy faithful

      Several of those open models were trained on the closed frontier's outputs. A Chinese open model is the closest thing to a readable copy of a closed US one.

      3

      The window is narrowing

      Flagships are going API-only. The time to calibrate open readers against closed authors is while frontier-adjacent weights are still downloadable.
